International Seminar on
Automorphic Forms

The seminar takes place on Tuesdays, usually from 16:00 to 17:00 CET (GMT+1, time zone of Amsterdam, Berlin, Rome, Stockholm, Vienna) via Zoom. The meetings start 15 minutes before the talk with a short coffee break.

You can join the Zoom meeting at https://tu-darmstadt.zoom.us/j/68048280736

The password is the first Fourier coefficient of the modular $j$-function (as digits).

27.10.2026 -- 16:00-17:00 (CET, UTC+1)
Campbell Wheeler (IHES)
Real quadratic fields and quantum dilogarithms

I will explain why the failure of modularity of the infinite Pochhammer symbol (half of the Jacobi theta-function) gives algebraic values at certain algebraic arguments. The proof follows from the construction of an explicit and overdetermined 0-dimensional algebraic variety where these values lie. (A detailed analysis of this variety gives rise to a complete answer to Hilbert’s 12th problem for real quadratic fields.) This is based on joint work with Danylo Radchenko.
